Alchemy — Trait Completion Plan

This is Aldren's core purpose. Aldren looks at the ingredient traits your character already knows, identifies the trait slots still missing, and suggests what ingredient or recipe you should make next to complete Alchemy trait discovery.

Use this when you are stuck or do not want to craft randomly. The plan compares known reagent traits against missing traits, then points you toward useful experiments that can reveal the next undiscovered trait.

Glyph Recipes

Every glyph recipe begins with the same three-part structure:

Potency Rune + Essence Rune + Aspect Rune

Potency shapes the level and direction of the glyph, Essence gives the glyph its meaning, and Aspect governs the strength of the result.
